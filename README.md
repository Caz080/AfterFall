# AFTERFALL
Made for the Knight Hacks Hackathon 2026

<img width="2287" height="1335" alt="AlienAttack" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9878d19d-cb9e-43da-b6e5-11ddf81dab47" />

In Afterfall, you are sat in the detective's chair. Six suspects sit before you: five humans and one disguised alien. Your only tools are your voice and your intuition.

* **Speak naturally** Interrogate suspects out loud, with no menus or scripted dialogue options.
* **Face convincingly human NPCs** Every suspect responds in the moment, so no two playthroughs are ever the same.
* **Hear them, too** Each suspect has a distinct, **AI generated** voice that gives them a personality you can read, doubt, and question.

<img width="2287" height="1335" alt="AlienResponseImg" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f90e361f-31b5-4fb1-8580-d81d80aa6197" />

The result is a fully immersive mystery where your questions and intuition are life or death.

* A glimpse of the **future** of gaming: characters you can actually talk to and answer like people instead of scripted dialogue trees.
